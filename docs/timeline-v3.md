## Preserved and changed

Preserved: customer and Studio layouts, conversations, typing, file-tile upload motion, clarification exchange, engineering edits, same office model, logo treatment, Inter UI/body family, espresso/leaf palette. Added optional shell-only presentation props for handoffs; defaults preserve the archived compositions.

Changed: exact website opening headline and upload line; spatial opening-to-Works handoff; explicit Works-to-Studio explanation; removal of the redundant 17-second middle animation; drawing-to-package handoff; distinct electrical/lighting thumbnails; model preview; richer deliverable formats; ZIP convergence/progress/checkmark; connected brand ending.

The saved time funds the new delivery handoff and richer download sequence while keeping the film at 120 seconds. The first prototype and v2 remain registered for reference; `FullDemoV3` is now the first composition in Studio.

## Production interpretation

This remains an illustrative product reconstruction with authored engineering graphics. No real backend upload, calculation, professional approval, Revit publication or customer download is executed by rendering. Implementation details and roadmap distinctions are documented privately in [delivery evidence](internal/delivery-evidence-v3.md), rather than repeated as internal footnotes in the film.
