## Treatment: A building, becoming legible

A warm, oblique architectural drawing gradually settles toward a readable plan. The camera movement relaxes as information gains structure. Room regions appear in sequence; relationship lines connect them; an unresolved room remains warm ochre. Linear luminaires populate a spatial array with small, damped arrival rings. A clipped soft field then makes the proposed distribution visible. The model dissolves into the actual logo and proposed closing line.

Use architectural drafting detail rather than decorative particles: dimension ticks, furniture, slab edge, room boundaries, sparse connections, fixture symbols and north arrow. Keep explanation left, engineering right. Avoid neon, stock footage, fake code, compliant/pass badges and fabricated UI activity.

Motion is deterministic and frame-driven at 30 fps: cubic-bezier easing, restrained perspective/rotation, staggered interpretation and placement, 0.5–0.7 s title arrivals/fades. The building remains continuous across the first four beats; this prevents four unrelated slides. Five named timeline sequences expose the narrative for editing.

Sound: original low harmonic bed and restrained pitched accents at 0/3/6/9/12 s, generated by `scripts/sound.py`. No stock recordings, paid generation, voiceover or copyrighted soundtrack. Source and WAV travel with the project.

The prototype is a visual-direction study. Field gradients are stylized and explicitly illustrative. A final film should replace them with an approved, provenance-bound Radiance example if numerical claims are desired. The closing tagline remains proposed copy pending creative review.
